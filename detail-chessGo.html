Chess Go

A chess-like strategy runner, published on CafeBazaar

Overview
  • Category Unity
  • Start date September 2021
  • Build time 4 months, solo
  • Project URL CafeBazaar

Chess Go is a chess-like strategy game combined with the endless runner genre and some other twists. It is my first published game, made with Unity.

I made the game all by myself in the span of 4 months, without using any premade assets except for sounds — everything else was built from scratch. Blender for 3D modelling, UV editing and texturing; Aseprite or Figma for the 2D sprites; Audacity for sound editing. The teaser was made entirely in Blender, posters and UI prototypes in Figma, and I managed it all in Trello.

One of the most challenging parts was the AI, which I programmed based on a minimax approach. I also implemented Unity's new Analytics SDK, CafeBazaar's in-app purchase and used Tapsell for ads.

Chess Go was published on CafeBazaar, the biggest app market in Iran, and was chosen by CafeBazaar to be listed on the home page.
