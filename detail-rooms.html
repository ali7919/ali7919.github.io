Rooms

A first-person puzzle game built with HDRP

Overview
  • Category Unity — HDRP
  • Start date June 2022
  • Role Solo developer

Rooms is a first-person puzzle game made with Unity. I chose HDRP for better visuals and a more photo-realistic look.

I'm its solo developer, and except for the forest, the rest of the models were also made by me in Blender.
