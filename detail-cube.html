Rubik Cube

A simulated 3D Rubik's cube

Overview
  • Category Unity
  • Start date January 2020

A simulated 3D Rubik's cube made with Unity.

Every face of the cube has a black button on it; clicking the button rotates that face 90 degrees. Each face is made of 9 smaller cubies — the right one is picked up on click and rotated 90 degrees around the centre cubie of that face.
