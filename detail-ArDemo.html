AR Demo

Two augmented-reality experiments in Unity

Overview
  • Category Unity — AR
  • Start date April 2022
  • Demos 2 prototypes

Two demo projects around Unity AR.

  • Boss fight — a simple encounter that starts with a sequence: the player opens a chest full of gems, then the boss appears with one attack type (shooting missiles) and the player can shoot back at it. The player, the boss and its missiles all have health that decreases when they're hit.
  • AR credit card — the card spawns on the scanned image of the actual credit card and sticks to it. The visuals are achieved using shaders and the stencil buffer.
